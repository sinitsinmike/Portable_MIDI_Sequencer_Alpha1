# Alpha Sequencer — One Page Pitch

## The Problem
Modern music tools are powerful but disconnected from performance.

- DAWs are visual and slow
- Step sequencers break musical flow
- Hardware often imitates software

Musicians end up programming instead of playing.

---

## The Solution
Alpha is a **performance-first MIDI sequencer**.

It lets you:
- play in real time
- capture musical ideas instantly
- stay in flow without screens

---

## What Makes It Different

### 🎹 Play-Centric Design
No piano roll. No step grid.  
You **perform the sequence**, not draw it.

---

### ⚡ Deterministic Timing
- Tight internal clock
- Predictable behavior
- Built for live performance

---

### 🎛 Direct Hardware Interaction
- Buttons + encoder
- Immediate response
- No deep menus

---

### 🔁 Flexible Recording
- Linear (full performance capture)
- Loop (layered overdub)

---

### 🌐 Hybrid Workflow
- Works standalone
- Connects to DAW when needed
- Clean MIDI roundtrip

---

## Why It Matters

Alpha is not another sequencer.

It is:
> A hardware instrument for capturing musical intent in real time.

---

## Target Users

- Live performers
- DAWless musicians

---

## Vision

Alpha aims to redefine sequencing as:
- performance
- timing
- interaction

Not editing.

## Real-Time MIDI Looper

Alpha can also be used as a **real-time MIDI looper**.

Similar to an audio looper, but for MIDI:

- Play → record → layer → repeat
- Build musical structures live
- No step programming
- No timeline editing

Unlike typical loopers:
- No forced quantization
- No hidden timing correction
- What you play is what you get

This makes Alpha feel closer to an **instrument** than a sequencer.
